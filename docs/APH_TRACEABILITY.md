## 3. Routes

**Chapter I: Scrutiny → Re-Scrutiny → EVC** (`CH1_NEW_INSTITUTION`). APH: APH Ch. I cl. 1.8–1.10 (printed pp. 7–8); Annexure 7 (p. 100)  
Stages: Scrutiny Committee (14 d) → Re-Scrutiny Committee (10 d, conditional) → Expert Visit Committee (14 d)

**Self-disclosure** (`SELF_DISCLOSURE`). APH: APH Table 2.3 rows 1–2 (printed p. 30); cl. 2.1  
Stages: Self-disclosure check (10 d)

**Scrutiny only** (`SCRUTINY_ONLY`). APH: APH Table 2.3 (printed p. 30); cl. 1.8, 2.23  
Stages: Scrutiny Committee (14 d) → Re-Scrutiny Committee (10 d, conditional)

**Scrutiny + EVC (+ SHC as applicable)** (`SCRUTINY_EVC_CONDITIONAL`). APH: APH Table 2.3 (printed p. 30); cl. 2.23 (p. 29)  
Stages: Scrutiny Committee (14 d) → Re-Scrutiny Committee (10 d, conditional) → Expert Visit Committee (14 d) → Standing Hearing Committee (14 d, conditional)

**EVC before first batch passes out** (`REG_ONLY_EVC`). REGULATION: Grant of Approval Regs 2024-27 cl. 6.2(j)  
Stages: Expert Visit Committee (14 d) → Standing Hearing Committee (14 d, conditional)

**Inspection + SHC hearing (show cause / complaint)** (`REG_EVC_SHC`). REGULATION: Grant of Approval Regs 2024-27 cl. 6.3(b), 6.11, 11.2  
Stages: Expert Visit Committee (14 d) → Standing Hearing Committee (14 d)

**Appeal to the Standing Appellate Committee** (`APPEAL_SAC`). APH: APH cl. 2.24 (p. 29) for existing institutions; cl. 1.11 (p. 9) for new  
Stages: Standing Appellate Committee (14 d)

**Manual rule review** (`MANUAL_RULE_REVIEW`). DESIGN: Blueprint §3.1: unmapped categories → MANUAL_RULE_REVIEW  
Stages: Rule review (10 d)

Stage time limits are DESIGN defaults: the APH fixes the sequence of committees, not their durations.

## 5. Checklists

**ANNEX2_EOA**. APH Annexure 2, cl. 2.1 (printed p. 81)  
Affidavit 2 (authenticated / digitally signed), Payment receipt, if any *(optional)*, Occupancy / Completion Certificate, Building Licence or Form D, Structural Stability Certificate (building older than 30 years) *(building over 30)*, Valid Fire Safety Certificate from the competent authority, Satellite / location map with latitude and longitude of the main entrance, Previous year's audited statement of accounts

**ANNEX2_GENERAL**. APH Annexure 2 (printed p. 81 onward)  
Affidavit 2 (authenticated / digitally signed), Documents prescribed in Annexure 2 for this category, Structural Stability Certificate (building older than 30 years) *(building over 30)*

**ANNEX2_NOC**. APH Annexure 2, cl. 2.3  
Affidavit 2 (authenticated / digitally signed), No Objection Certificate from the affiliating body / State Government, as applicable, Resolution of the applicant organisation, Structural Stability Certificate (building older than 30 years) *(building over 30)*

**ANNEX2_SITE**. APH Annexure 2, cl. 2.3; change of site also requires all Annexure 1 documents  
Affidavit 2 (authenticated / digitally signed), No Objection Certificate from the affiliating body / State Government, as applicable, Resolution of the applicant organisation, Approved building plan for the new site (Annexure 1), Land ownership documents or registered lease for the new site (Annexure 1), Supporting evidence for leased land (lessor's title, registration) *(land leased)*, Valid Fire Safety Certificate for the new site

**ANNEX1**. APH Annexure 1 (printed pp. 77–80)  
Approved building plan and architect / authority certificates, Affidavit 2 (authenticated / digitally signed), Registration document of the Society / Trust / Section-8 Company, Resolution allocating land, building and funds, Occupancy / Completion Certificate or applicable building document, Valid Fire Safety Certificate, Bank manager's certificate of financial status, Land ownership documents or registered lease (minimum 30 years), Supporting evidence for leased land (lessor's title, registration) *(land leased)*
